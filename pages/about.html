Project Name

Food Waste Reduction and Donation System — a university Internet Programming project.

Developed as part of the HTML stage of the course curriculum.

Background

Large amounts of edible food are wasted every day by restaurants, cafes, bakeries, supermarkets, and individual households. At the same time, many people in communities lack reliable access to sufficient food. This mismatch between surplus food and people in need is a significant social and environmental problem.

Food waste contributes to greenhouse gas emissions when organic matter decomposes in landfills.

Purpose

The purpose of this system is to provide a platform where food donors can register surplus food donations, track food availability and freshness, and coordinate the delivery of donated food to distribution centers so it reaches people who need it instead of being thrown away.

Main Objectives

  • Reduce food waste by redirecting surplus edible food to people in need.
  • Allow donors to register and manage food donations easily.
  • Track food availability, freshness, and delivery status in a structured way.
  • Prioritize urgent food donations that require fast delivery.
  • Support reporting on total, available, assigned, delivered, and urgent food.

How the Donation Process Works

  1. A donor registers an account or logs in with existing credentials.
  2. The donor registers a food donation, including donor name, source, food name, quantity, freshness, and expiry date.
  3. The donation is recorded and listed in the donations overview.
  4. Available and urgent food items are assigned to distribution centers and delivery drivers.
  5. Assigned food items are marked as delivered once they reach the distribution center.
  6. Summary reports show the totals for registered, available, assigned, delivered, and urgent food.

Note: The actual data processing behind these steps will be implemented in the Java stage.

Technical Notes

Food measurements may use scientific notation:
for example, carbon dioxide is written as CO2,
and food volume can be measured in cm3.

Food inventory follows the first-in, first-out principle to minimize waste.

When a food record is corrected, the old value is shown as struck through and replaced:

Freshness: Urgent Freshness: Fresh

Benefits

For Donors

Donors can reduce waste, manage surplus food responsibly, and keep track of what they have donated.

For Communities

Communities gain a structured channel for surplus food to reach people who need it, reducing the amount of edible food that ends up as waste.

For Food Recipients

Food recipients receive donated food through distribution centers, with urgent items prioritized for faster delivery.

Project Mission

To build a simple, accessible platform that connects food donors with distribution centers and people in need, helping reduce food waste and support food security in the community. This is a university project developed in stages; it is not yet a live operating system and has no organizational partnerships.
